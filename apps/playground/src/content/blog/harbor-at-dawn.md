The first boats go out around five. By the time the sun clears the breakwater, most of the fleet is a line of dots on the horizon and the harbor is quiet again.

## What to bring

- A warm layer, even in September.
- Something to sit on. The concrete stays cold until mid-morning.
- Patience. The best light lasts about ten minutes.

## Getting there

Park by the old customs house and follow the [harbor path](/locations/) east. The walk to the lighthouse takes twenty minutes at an easy pace.
