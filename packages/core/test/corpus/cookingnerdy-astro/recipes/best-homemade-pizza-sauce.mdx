---
title: Best Homemade Pizza Sauce
image: ../../assets/images/recipes/best-homemade-pizza-sauce/image.jpg
summary: >-
  This pizza sauce will beat any you get from the store and any you’ll have on a
  chain restaurant pizza. It is easy, robust, a little zesty, and comes together
  in about 20 minutes.
servings:
  number_servings: 4
  servings_unit: 15" to 16" pizzas
times:
  prep_time:
    minutes: 10
  cook_time:
    minutes: 15
  total_time:
    minutes: 25
courses:
  - condiments
cuisines:
  - american
equipment: []
ingredients:
  - amount: 28
    unit: oz
    name: whole peeled tomatoes
    notes: san marzano or similar
directions:
  - text: >-
      Empty the 28 oz whole peeled tomatoes into a large bowl and squish them
      with your hands to break them up into very small pieces. Something about
      this process is very satisfying. Believe me. Set aside.
  - text: >-
      Measure your 15 grams sugar, 9 grams sea salt, 0.3 grams red pepper flakes
      and 0.4 grams dried oregano and set aside.
  - text: >-
      Add your 20 grams olive oil to a large pot over medium heat. Sautee the 2
      cloves garlic until fragrant (about 30 seconds).
  - text: >-
      Dump your tomatoes, sugar, salt, oregano, and red pepper flakes in and
      combine.
  - text: >-
      Bring the pot to a simmer and simmer until reduced and thickened to your
      liking, about 15-20 minutes, stirring occassionally. Remove from the heat
      and allow to cool.
---
