---
title: Decarboxylate
categories:
  - Glossary
---

The process of removing one or more carboxyl groups from a molecule. When we cook with weed, we want to convert THCA into THC so that we achieve the desired effect when ingesting our cannabis creations. Also referred to as decarb or decarbing.
