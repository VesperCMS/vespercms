Indica is one of the three categories of cannabis, along with [Sativa](/glossary/sativa) and [Ruderalis](/glossary/ruderalis). Most Indicas provide a relaxed body high, which many enjoy for anxiety, sleep, pain, and more. Indica plants are often shorter, wider, and produce heavier buds than their Sativa relatives.
