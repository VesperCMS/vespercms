---
title: Trichome
categories:
  - Glossary
---

Trichomes are the glands of a cannabis plant that produce resin. Under magnification, they look like glassy little mushrooms covering the surface of the flowers and some leaves. They're very sticky and carry the vast majority of [cannabinoids](/glossary/cannabinoid) (like [THC](/glossary/thc), [CBD](/glossary/cbd) and [terpenes](/glossary/terpene)).
