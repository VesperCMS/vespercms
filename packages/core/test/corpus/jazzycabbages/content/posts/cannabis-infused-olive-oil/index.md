Cannabis-infused olive oil, or cannaoil, is one of the staple infusions in the cannabis kitchen. Infused oil works in nearly any recipe that calls for olive oil. Or, enjoy it drizzled on pizza, as a dipping medium for bread, or have a spoonful.

If you're new to edibles, check out our [cannabis edibles for beginners guide](/101) to learn the basics.

If you're looking for butter, check out our [cannabis-infused butter how-to](/butter).

## How Much Decarbed Weed Should I Use?

You always want to use decarboxylated cannabis for your infusions. See our [cannabis decarb how-to](/decarb/) to learn how to decarb your material.

- **Flower:** Try 14 to 28 grams of decarbed weed per cup of oil.
- **Concentrates:** Try 3 to 6 grams decarbed concentrate per cup of oil. To infuse, gently heat your oil and mix your decarbed concentrate directly into the oil. **Never microwave your cannabis products!**
- **Kief:** Try 7 to 14 grams of decarbed cannabis per cup of oil.
- **Trim and Shake:** Try 28 to 48 grams, decarbed per cup of oil.

For help with dosing, check out our [cannabis edibles dosing calculator](/calculator/).

### Note on Oil Loss

When infusing oil with flowers, some oil will stay in the bud, meaning some loss of your original quantity of oil. For this reason, I will often compensate by using 2.25 cups for each cup of oil I'm trying to make.

## How to Make Cannabis-Infused Olive Oil in a Crockpot

1. Lay a clean [tea towel](https://amzn.to/3kSAD4f) in the bottom of your [crockpot](https://amzn.to/3kb7elE) to buffer the glass jar against the bottom of the crock. Add enough water to the crockpot to come at least 3/4 of the way to the top of your jar(s).
2. Stick a digital thermometer in your water bath and turn the crockpot on high. Allow the water bath to come up to 185°F and then turn to low.
3. While the crockpot is heating up, decarb your cannabis if you haven't already. Add the oil and decarboxylated cannabis to a clean mason jar and screw on the lid finger tight.
4. Place your sealed jar in the crock and cook on low for four hours, maintaining a temperature of 185°F.
5. After four hours strain your oil and weed through a fine-mesh strainer/cheesecloth into a clean container or jar. See our [straining notes](#straining-cannabis-infused-olive-oil) below.
6. Allow the oil to cool.
7. Enjoy your oil, use it in [recipes](/categories/recipes/), or see notes below for [storage information](#storing-cannabis-infused-oil).

## How to Make Cannabis-Infused Olive Oil on a Stovetop

1. Combine your cannabis, oil, and two cups of water in a pot and bring to a gentle simmer. The water helps buffer temperature fluctuations. Take care not to exceed 210°F to preserve precious [cannabinoids](/glossary/cannabinoid/). A [candy thermometer](https://amzn.to/33DwHdS) helps monitor this. Around 200°F is the goal.
2. Gently simmer your material for four hours.
3. Strain your oil into a container and allow it to cool. See our [straining notes](#straining-cannabis-infused-olive-oil) below.
4. Enjoy your oil, use it in [recipes](/categories/recipes/), or see [storage notes](#storing-cannabis-infused-oil) below.

## How to Make Cannabis-Infused Olive Oil in an Infusion Device

You can simplify the infusion process by using an infusion machine such as the [LEVO](https://www.shareasale.com/r.cfm?b=1309195&u=3290418&m=85525), Ardent FX, or Magical Butter. These machines make infusion as easy as loading your cannabis and butter/oil into the chamber and pressing a button. The [LEVO](https://www.shareasale.com/r.cfm?b=1309195&u=3290418&m=85525) and Ardent can even decarb your cannabis!

## Straining Cannabis-Infused Olive Oil

Straining the plant material from your oil helps create a more pure, longer-lasting, better-tasting product. You'll want to use something food-safe like [unbleached coffee filters](https://amzn.to/2U02Lrb) and a [funnel](https://amzn.to/3yQwBgK), a [fine-mesh strainer](https://amzn.to/3hzg4ba), [cheesecloth](https://amzn.to/3AT0ZbZ), or my personal favorite: a [25-micron bubble bag](https://amzn.to/3yLzqiY). I like the bubble bags best because they don't absorb nearly much of my prized oil as cheesecloth or a coffee filter is prone to absorbing.

## Storing Cannabis-Infused Oil

Store your olive oil in an air-tight container in the fridge for a month or freeze for several months. Cannabinoids do degrade over time, so it is best to use your oil soon.
