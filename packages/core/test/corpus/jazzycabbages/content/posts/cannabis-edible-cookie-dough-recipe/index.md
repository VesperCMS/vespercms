Everyone has eaten their fair share of raw cookie dough, but the eggs are not the only potentially unsafe ingredient when consuming regular raw cookie dough. Plain flour can harbor bacteria such as E. coli, so for this recipe, we heat-treat the flour in the oven to kill any unwanted bacteria for safer cookie dough. It's easy and results in a safe, medicated sweet treat!
