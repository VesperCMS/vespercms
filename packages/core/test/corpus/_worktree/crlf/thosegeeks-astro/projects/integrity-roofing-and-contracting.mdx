## The Challenge

Alan at Integrity Roofing and Construction in Christiansburg, Virginia got in touch with us to help finish their company website project started by another developer. The foundations were there, but he needed help smoothing things out and wrapping it up to get it production-ready. Things were behind, so we needed to move quickly.

## The Solution

While the site was off to a good start, we decided to convert the layout to Elementor so that we could move fast and still create a great-looking experience for Integrity and its potential customers. Alan already had what he wanted in mind for the homepage and we built that and built out services, a blog, and geographically specific landing pages to help with organic search engine placement. Integrity was super quick in delivering content, and we were super quick to get the site ready to go. We were able to get it done in just a couple of weeks.
