The old site was put together using their cataloging software, which was clunky and difficult to create a good user experience with. Many sections were difficult to navigate, the URL and pages weren’t search engine optimized, and the design tools weren’t there. We met with key library staff and worked together to develop a vision for the new site.

We built the new site on WordPress, making it easy for library staff at all three locations to log in and add events, slides, news posts, and more as needed. Our custom theme ensures visual consistency across the site. We also worked with SCPL to convert several essential library forms into online forms sent to the appropriate staff member. The library staff took to using their new freedom immediately, and the library’s site is updated almost daily.
