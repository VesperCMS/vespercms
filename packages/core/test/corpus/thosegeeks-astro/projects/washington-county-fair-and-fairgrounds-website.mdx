## The Challenge

The [Washington County Fair website](https://washcofair.com/) was outdated, hard to edit, and challenging to maintain. Its visuals were several years old and the site was not mobile-optimized, making an update necessary. That’s where ThoseGeeks came in.

## The Solution

We kept the site on WordPress because the staff was already familiar with the platform. First, we overhauled the global color scheme and font system, and developed a mobile-friendly structure for the site. For pages needing a more interesting layout, we used Elementor, while for most content pages, we used the WordPress core block editor. This resulted in a much-improved site that we will continue to enhance year over year.
