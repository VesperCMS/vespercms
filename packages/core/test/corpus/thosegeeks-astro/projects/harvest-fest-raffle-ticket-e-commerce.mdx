ASD needed a solution fast since the tickets were already on sale, so they came to ThoseGeeks for help as we had helped build their website and e-commerce already.

## **The Solution**

ASD’s website was already on WordPress with working e-commerce, so we quickly duplicated their order form and tickets using GravityForms and hooked things up with their Stripe account so customers could order straight from the HarvestFest page on their site. ASD receives the order information so they can run the raffle. The money goes straight to ASD’s bank. Customers aren’t hit with extra fees. We had the order form ready to go in under 24 hours. Wins all around!
